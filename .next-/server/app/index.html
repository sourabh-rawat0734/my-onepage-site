The Problem

Health advice isn't personal.

Every day we're told what to eat, how to exercise and how to live healthier. Yet much of this advice is based on population averages, not on you.

Because your biology is unique, the same lifestyle can lead to very different outcomes for different people.

Gut Microbiome Deep Sequencing Data

Utilizing targeted laboratory diagnostics to parse bacterial architecture and mapping individual specific strain performance metrics

Lifestyle & Behavioral Data Points

Integrating structured intake logs covering accurate dietary patterns, real-world metabolic expenditure, stress indices, and circadian rhythms.

European Cohort Predictive AI Engine

Advanced machine learning pattern recognition trained on verified Western European population health cohorts to avoid generic averages.

Our Solution

Understand yourself. Improve what matters.

HOW IT WORKS

Your journey to personalized health

  • 1. Tell us about yourself

    Complete a short questionnaire about your health, lifestyle, and goals on the SymVentra platform.

  • 2. Track your daily habits

    Use the SymVentra platform for one week to log your meals, sleep, movement and overall well being.

  • 3. Add your microbiome

    Order your microbiome test, collect your stool sample at home using our easy-to-use collection kit, and send it to our trusted laboratory partner. Once the analysis is complete, your results will be available in the SymVentra platform, providing deeper biological insights.

  • 4. Discover your patterns

    Our platform combines your biology and lifestyle data to identify meaningful patterns and connections.

  • 5. Receive personalised guidance

    Get practical recommendations designed around you, not the average person.

  • 6. Learn over time

    Continue tracking your lifestyle each week and repeat your home stool test every three months. As your biology changes, your insights and recommendations evolve with you.

Every great innovation starts with a simple question.

What if health advice could be as unique as the person receiving it?

Inspired by years of microbiome research and a passion for preventive health, SymVentra began as an idea to make personalized health more accessible. Through entrepreneurship, scientific collaboration, customer validation and public innovation programmes, that idea gradually evolved into a growing health technology company. Along the way, we have been supported by leading innovation partners, secured competitive grants, expanded our multidisciplinary team and validated our platform in real-world settings.

Today, SymVentra is building the next generation of personalized health by combining biology, lifestyle and scientific evidence, helping people better understand themselves and make more informed choices for a healthier future.
